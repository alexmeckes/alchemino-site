Alchemino: Block Puzzle

Support

Place blocks, clear lines and ripen lead into gold. Stages 1 and 2 are free; one in-app purchase unlocks the whole game.

Contact

Email alex@hugebrain.ai. If something went wrong, say which phone you play on. Testers: paste the game log too (Settings › Copy game log).

Restore your purchase

The unlock usually comes back by itself on a new phone or after reinstalling. If it does not, open Settings › Unlock full game and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
