Alchemino: Block Puzzle

Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Alchemino collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything to us.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your settings, your run in progress, whether you have unlocked the full game, and a play log on your device. The play log records game events, such as rounds played and screens opened, so problems can be traced. It never leaves your device unless you send it to us yourself (below).

Your own cloud account

On Apple devices, if iCloud is on, your run in progress is also stored in your own iCloud account so you can continue on another device. On Android, the system backup may copy your settings and progress to your own Google account. We cannot see either copy.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details and no personal information from it, only whether the full game is unlocked on your device.

If you contact us

If you email us, or choose to copy the game log and send it to us (for example as a tester), we use what you send only to answer you and to fix problems, and we do not share it.

Children

Alchemino is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
