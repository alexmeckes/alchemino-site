Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Alchemino has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The only thing it sends us is anonymous play stats, and you can turn them off in the game.

Play stats

To learn how the game is played and where it can be better, the game sends us anonymous play stats. They record things like a run starting, the stage and round it reached and how it ended, the purchase screen being shown and what was chosen on it, and how long a session lasted. Each stat comes with the platform (iOS or Android), the game's build number, the week the game was first opened, the number of days since then, and whether the full game is unlocked.

Play stats contain no name, email address, account, advertising identifier, device identifier or any other ID, so we cannot tell which stats came from you or connect them to a person. We do not store your IP address, your location, your device model or the time of day. We do not use play stats for advertising, combine them with data from other apps or websites, or sell or share them.

Play stats are received by a server we run on Cloudflare, which hosts it for us. Cloudflare handles your device's IP address to deliver each request and to block abuse; we do not keep it. We keep play stats for 13 months and then delete them.

To turn play stats off: open Settings, tap Privacy, then Turn off play stats. The game stops sending them at once and deletes any it has not sent yet. You can turn them back on the same way.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your settings, your run in progress, whether you have unlocked the full game, and a play log on your device. The play log records game events, such as rounds played and screens opened, so problems can be traced. The game itself never sends the play log; it leaves your device only if you copy it and send it to us yourself (below). For play stats, the game also keeps the day it was first opened and which one-time stats it has already sent.

Your own cloud account

On Apple devices, if iCloud is on, your run in progress is also stored in your own iCloud account so you can continue on another device. On Android, the system backup may copy your settings and progress to your own Google account. We cannot see either copy.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details and no personal information from it. Whether a purchase or restore succeeded is part of the anonymous play stats above, if they are on.

If you contact us

If you email us, or choose to copy the game log and send it to us (for example as a tester), we use what you send only to answer you and to fix problems, and we do not share it.

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK

Huge Brain Labs, LLC is responsible for play stats. We process them because we have a legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can object at any time by turning them off. Because play stats cannot be linked to you, we usually cannot find the stats that came from you in order to show or delete them. You can contact us with any question, and you have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Alchemino is not directed at children under 13. Play stats are anonymous, and the game collects no personal data from anyone.
